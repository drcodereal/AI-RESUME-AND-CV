Privacy policy.

How ResumeAI Studio collects, uses, and protects your data. Last updated 3 DAYS AGO.

On this page 1. Information we collect 2. How we use it 3. Storage & retention 4. Sharing your data 5. Your rights 6. Cookies 7. Contact

1. Information we collect

When you use ResumeAI Studio we collect the details you enter into the resume builder (name, contact info, work history, skills), the resume files you upload for analysis, and basic account information if you register.

2. How we use it

Your data is used only to generate resumes, run ATS analysis, and produce job-match scoring on your behalf. We do not use your resume content to train external models or sell it to advertisers.

3. Storage & retention

Generated resumes and analysis reports are stored in your account history so you can revisit them later. You can delete individual items at any time from the History page; deleted items are removed from our database.

4. Sharing your data

We do not sell or rent your personal data. Limited data may be shared with infrastructure providers (hosting, database) strictly to operate the service, under standard confidentiality terms.

5. Your rights

You can request a copy of your data, ask us to correct inaccuracies, or request full deletion of your account and associated resumes/analyses at any time by contacting us.

6. Cookies

We use minimal, functional cookies to keep you signed in and remember your preferences. We do not use third-party advertising trackers.

7. Contact

Questions about this policy? Reach out via the contact details on our homepage.
